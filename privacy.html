Privacy Policy

Elemental Arena is a free browser game. This page explains what data the game stores when you sign in with Google.

What we store

  • Your Google account ID and email address, which are used only to sign you in (handled by Supabase Auth).
  • The nickname you choose, which is shown publicly on the leaderboard.
  • Your game progress (gems, skins, quests, achievements) and your run results (stage, time, hero, win/loss).

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We don't show ads or use tracking cookies.
  • Your email is never shown to other players.

Deleting your data

You can play without signing in. To delete your account and all saved data, contact the developer through the game's GitHub page.
